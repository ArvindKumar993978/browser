Orbit Browser — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2026

Zero Telemetry Guarantee: Orbit Browser is local-first. We do not collect, sell, or transmit your browsing history, bookmarks, searches, or device identifiers to any developer-controlled servers.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Orbit Browser has no telemetry servers, no usage analytics, and no automatic crash-upload beacons.
  • We never track the websites you visit, your search queries, or your downloads.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Orbit stores data locally inside an SQLite database (%APPDATA%\orbit-browser\orbit.db on Windows):

  • Browsing History & Session Tabs: URLs, page titles, and timestamps from normal windows.
  • Bookmarks & Workspaces: Pages you star and your workspace configuration.
  • Downloads: File names, source URLs, save paths, and transfer status.
  • Site Permissions & Adblock Allowlist: Your Allow/Block choices for camera, microphone, location, notifications, and per-site shield switches.
  • Settings: HTTPS-First mode, Vertical Tabs, and tab sleeping preferences.

You can wipe any or all of this anytime from Privacy & Shield → Clear Browsing Data.

3. Private Windows

  • Private Windows (Ctrl+Shift+N) use an isolated, in-memory partition.
  • Orbit never writes Private Window URLs to history or session restore, never auto-grants saved permissions, and clears cookies/cache when the Private Window closes.

4. Network Connections Made by Orbit

  • Websites you visit (subject to each website's own privacy policy).
  • Search queries typed in the address bar (sent to DuckDuckGo by default).
  • Ad & Tracker Filter Initialization: Fetches Ghostery's prebuilt filter list once and caches it locally (adblocker-engine.bin).
  • App Update Checks: Installed builds check GitHub Releases (github.com/ArvindKumar993978/browser) for newer versions and only download updates when you choose to download.
